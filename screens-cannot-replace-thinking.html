Screens Cannot Replace Students' Thinking

A keynote speaker and journalism coach, Xydrone Cawaling, warns that excessive dependence on artificial intelligence (AI) may weaken students' critical thinking, creativity, and reading comprehension, urging schools to prioritize responsible AI use and independent learning.

A keynote speaker raised concerns about students becoming overly dependent on AI, warning that it may affect their ability to think critically and learn independently.

The speaker emphasized that copying AI-generated answers without understanding them can encourage academic dishonesty and limit students' creativity.

However, AI can help explain difficult lessons and provide additional learning support when used responsibly.

The speaker also stressed the importance of protecting students' personal information when using AI tools.

Schools were encouraged to promote textbook reading, independent writing, and critical thinking to strengthen students' learning skills.

Teachers and school administrators must work together to ensure that technology supports education rather than replaces traditional learning methods.

The speaker concluded that AI should remain a learning tool, reminding students that genuine understanding and independent effort are essential to academic success.
