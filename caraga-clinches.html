Caraga Clinches 2-1 Win Over Bicol in Sepak Takraw Thriller at Palaro Tu Agsur 2026

Caraga secures a hard-earned victory after three closely contested sets.

AGUSAN DEL SUR — Caraga edged out Bicol in a pulsating three-set Sepak Takraw encounter, claiming a hard-earned 2-1 victory in front of a lively crowd at Palaro Tu Agsur 2026 on Tuesday. The final scores stood at 21-19, 18-21, and 21-17 after nearly an hour of high-flying action.

The opening set was tight from start to finish, with both teams exchanging powerful spikes and acrobatic saves that kept spectators on the edge of their seats. Caraga pulled ahead at crucial moments to take the first set, 21-19. Bicol quickly regrouped, showing impressive composure to dominate the second set and level the match at 1-1.

With everything on the line in the deciding set, the intensity increased as both sides refused to back down. Long rallies and spectacular defensive plays highlighted the action, but Caraga found an extra gear toward the end, pulling away to seal a 21-17 victory and take the match.

The match demonstrated the determination, athleticism, and teamwork of both regional squads as they battled for tournament victory.

Bicol, while disappointed with the result, remained gracious in defeat. “It was a close match and things just didn’t go our way in the final set. We’re proud of how we played, and this experience will only make us better when we meet again,” their team captain remarked after the final whistle.

The match was streamed live on the Palaro Tu Agsur 2nd Channel, reaching over 3,800 viewers who tuned in to support their respective regions.

The win boosts Caraga’s standing in the tournament, while both teams walk away having gained valuable experience and showcased the skill and sportsmanship of Philippine Sepak Takraw athletes.
