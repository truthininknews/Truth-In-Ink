HOW TO DEVELOP LIFE-CHANGING HABITS: SPEECH FROM THE NARRA

Guest speaker Xydrone Cawaling warns campus journalists about the effects of artificial intelligence on students' education.

Students' grades are improving, and assignments are being completed on time. But behind the scenes, a machine is providing the answers, potentially causing students to develop unhealthy habits.

Addressing the Online Publication Press Conference, guest speaker Xydrone Cawaling expressed concern about the younger generation's misuse of artificial intelligence. He urged students, teachers, administrators, and department heads to promote the responsible use of AI, allowing students to learn comfortably without developing unhealthy habits.

First, be conscious of your actions. Cawaling explained that the decisions you make can affect your future. Making the right decisions is the first step toward gaining knowledge through AI responsibly.

Second, being ethical in your heart and mind is another stepping stone. Many successful people possess this characteristic. Cawaling emphasized that developing this characteristic is another healthy habit one can practice when carrying out responsibilities.

Third, take accountability for your work and words Cawaling highlighted that taking responsibilities for your duties is one way that reflects good uses of artificial intelligence.

Fourth, think before you act, Cawaling noted that not all information that Ai extract is not 100% accurate which is why we need to double check information to avoid spreading false news.

Fifth, learn before you let Ai help whether if it's for research,assingment, or a group project. Learn for yourself to develop self thinking.And use only Ai for complex topics and problems.

Finally, keep the pace of all these healthly habits. Your academics and grades may stay the same but gaining a healthy pace for good reasons is better than receiving false hope. Following Cawalings guide and steps is the first path of avoiding irresponsibility and unethical work.

Whether its about report cards and grades - they do not determine our future,but rather it is ourselves who should forge a better path for all of us to keep using AI ethically and responsibly in the next generation of artificial intelligence.
